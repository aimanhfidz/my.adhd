Get assessed in Malaysia

Only a psychiatrist or a clinical psychologist can diagnose ADHD. There are two ways to reach one here, and both start with a single appointment.

Through a klinik kesihatan

  • See a doctor at your klinik kesihatan and ask for a referral letter to a hospital psychiatry clinic.
  • For citizens it is RM1 to register. With a government doctor’s referral, the first specialist visit is free and each follow-up is RM5.
  • The wait can be long, and adult clinics are mostly in the bigger hospitals.

Going private

  • Book a psychiatrist or a clinical psychologist directly. You don’t need a referral.
  • A full adult assessment at the clinics we looked at runs from about RM1,300 to RM3,800 (October 2026).
  • Ask what the fee covers: the report, the follow-up, any tests.

Psychiatrist or psychologist?

  • Both can diagnose. Only a psychiatrist can prescribe medication.
  • Check a psychiatrist on the Malaysian Medical Council’s register.
  • A clinical psychologist should be able to give you their MAHPC registration number.

What to bring

  • Your self-check result, printed or on your phone.
  • Notes on what actually goes wrong in a normal week.
  • Anything from childhood: old school reports, or someone who knew you then.

If today can’t wait: Talian Kasih 15999 or Befrienders 03-7627 2929, both 24 hours. In danger right now, call 999.

Start with something to bring.

Ten minutes, and a printout a psychiatrist or psychologist can read.

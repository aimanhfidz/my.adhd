## Description

Paste as it stands. The opening line is the site's own (`index.html`,
`about.html`); the closing tagline is BRAND.md §8.5, one per surface,
untranslated. The disclaimer is the Late Talks page's footer.

```
Too many Malaysians grow up believing they're careless, lazy, or just not trying hard enough. MyADHD exists to close that gap.

This channel is for adults with ADHD, and the people around them. Talks and conversations on what ADHD does to time, focus and feelings, and what helps, explained simply.

Recordings of my.adhd Late Talks, our online series, are here for ticket holders who add them, with lifetime access.

On myadhd.my: a free self-check, Living with ADHD, habits, a reading list and tools.

An educational channel. It is not a diagnosis or treatment. For a diagnosis, see a psychiatrist or clinical psychologist.

Understand. Connect. Thrive.
```

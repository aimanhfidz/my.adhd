ADHD is not just about “lack of focus”.

It shapes your emotions, energy, decisions and daily life.

Not because you're lazy. Your brain works differently.

So we made three evenings to talk about it Register free

my.adhd Late Talks

Exploring a holistic, contextual view of ADHD. Three Saturday nights on Zoom, 9:00–11:00 pm, with live Q&A.

One picture of ADHD, from three angles.

Before the series, and after it.

  • You don't fully understand why focus, attention and consistency are so hard for you.You understand how your ADHD brain works, and how it differs from other people's.
  • You often feel overwhelmed, reactive or drained, and you're not sure why.You have practical ways to handle emotions and the day's challenges.
  • You blame yourself for “not trying hard enough”, even when nobody has said it.You stop blaming yourself, and start working with your brain.
  • You feel alone, as if nobody gets what your days are like.You know you're not alone, and that there is a better way forward.

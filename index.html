TL;DR

What you need to know

Four quick things about adult ADHD in Malaysia.

Not just hyper

Adult ADHD often looks like overwhelm, daydreaming or losing focus — not the physical hyperactivity you see in children.

Not laziness

The struggle comes from differences in the brain’s executive function, not from a lack of discipline or motivation.

It can be managed

With a proper diagnosis, the right treatment and everyday strategies, adults with ADHD can live full, productive lives.

You are not alone

Many Malaysian adults live with ADHD without ever being diagnosed. The MyADHD community is open to you.

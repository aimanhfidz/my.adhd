The four beats

  • 01

    Mark

    00.00 – 00.70

    The full my.adhd asterisk. Seven orange arms plus the purple pill sitting in the empty south-east slot — the pill is the eighth arm, not a floating dot.

  • 02

    Attention

    02.00 – 02.70

    Arms detach from the centre and a solid core forms. The mark opens into a burst — scattered attention, all of it pointed inward.

  • 03

    Session

    04.00 – 04.70

    Arms shrink to tick marks around a ring; the purple arm drops to the centre and becomes the hand. A focus timer, built entirely from the logo.

  • 04

    Reset

    06.00 – 06.70

    Everything collapses tight and spins a half-turn. Energy discharges, then the arms extend back out and the mark reassembles at 08.00.

Nothing is drawn twice

Every beat is the same eight rectangles changing length, thickness and corner radius around a shared centre. That is why the transitions read as a true morph rather than a crossfade — and why the purple element never stops being the south-east arm.

Where each file goes

.svg animates on its own — drop it in the app as a splash or loading state. .mp4 for Threads and Reels. .gif for anywhere that won't take video.

Retiming it

The whole loop is driven by one value: dur="8s", repeated on every animate tag in the SVG. Find-and-replace it to speed up or slow down — the four beats stay proportional.

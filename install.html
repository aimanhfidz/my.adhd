One-time setup · 20 seconds

Put it on your home screen first.

my.adhd lives in the browser, but it’s meant to be reached the way you reach any app: one tap, no address bar, no tab to lose. Save it now and it behaves like one.

  • Opens full screen
  • One tap, no tab hunting
  • Works offline
  • Your own icon
  1. Open this page in Safari (Chrome on iPhone can’t do it).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The my.adhd icon is now on your home screen.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen (some phones call it Install app).
  3. Confirm with Install or Add.
  4. Find the my.adhd icon with the rest of your apps.
  1. In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click it, then Install. In Safari, use File → Add to Dock.
  3. my.adhd opens in its own window, no tabs around it.
Done — open my.adhd

Nothing is installed from a store and nothing is uploaded — the home-screen icon is a shortcut to this page, and everything you type stays in your browser.

Living with ADHD · Guilt about resting

Why can't I rest without feeling guilty?

You do not have to earn a break.

The short version

01

There is always something undone

The unanswered message, the laundry, the task you put off. With a long list in your head, a break feels like falling further behind.

02

Rest feels like slacking

Years of being called lazy make every pause feel like proof. So you rest the way you would skip class, tense and watching the door.

03

Scrolling is not rest

Hours on the phone can look like a break. Often you come out more tired. Your brain was busy the whole time, just not on anything you chose.

04

Your mind keeps working

Even on the sofa, the to-do list runs in the background. Your body is resting, but your head is still at work.

05

Book rest like a task

Put it in the calendar. 4pm to 5pm, rest. With a slot of its own, your brain treats it as allowed, not stolen.

06

Pick rest that fills you

A walk, a nap, music, cooking for fun. Notice which ones leave you better than before, and choose those on purpose.

07

Park the list first

Write down what is waiting before you stop. Tomorrow it will still be there. Right now it does not need you.

Rest is part of the work, not a reward for it. Dump the list into my.adhd and let it hold things while you put them down.

Living with ADHD · Hobby-hopping

Why do I quit every new hobby?

You are not flaky. New things just light you up.

The short version.

01

New things light up your brain

A fresh hobby is full of novelty. Every video, every tool, every first try feels exciting. That rush is real fuel for an ADHD brain.

02

Then the novelty fades

Week three, it is just practice. Same chords, same mistakes. The spark that carried you is gone, and nothing has replaced it yet.

03

The kit stays, the guilt grows

Paints in the cupboard, a guitar in the corner, a gym card in the wallet. Each one feels like proof you never finish anything.

04

You still learned something

Three months of anything is still three months. You picked up skills, met people and had fun. A hobby can end without being a failure.

05

Borrow before you buy

Rent it, borrow it, take one class first. Let the excitement settle before the money goes in.

06

Rotate instead of quitting

Keep two or three hobbies on a loop. When one goes stale, switch, and come back to it later. It will feel fresh again.

07

Do it with someone

A class, a club, a friend who expects you on Saturday. Other people keep a hobby going on the weeks the spark is low.

Loving new things is part of how your brain works. Dump the ideas into my.adhd and get one thing back to try this week.

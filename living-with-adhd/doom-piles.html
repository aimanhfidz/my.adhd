Living with ADHD · Doom piles

Why is there a pile on every surface?

It is not mess for the sake of mess. It is a filing system that got out of hand.

The short version.

01

Out of sight, out of mind

For many ADHD brains, what goes in a drawer stops existing. So the letter stays on the table, where you can see it. The pile is a reminder that grew.

02

Every item is a small decision

Keep, bin, file, reply, return. Each thing asks a question, and the pile turns twelve small questions into one huge one. Huge ones get avoided.

03

Tidying is boring with no deadline

No one is waiting for it. Nothing happens if you skip it today. Without interest or urgency, the pile has nothing to pull you in.

04

The shame makes it heavier

Every glance at the pile comes with a small "what is wrong with me". That feeling is the real reason you avoid the corner, not the paper.

05

Give it a home you can see

Open trays, clear boxes, hooks on the wall. If out of sight means gone, stop hiding things. Storage you can see is not mess, it is memory.

06

Five minutes, one pile

Not the whole room. One pile, one timer, three bags: keep, bin, somewhere else. Stop when it rings, even if you are not done.

07

One landing spot by the door

Keys, post, receipts, the thing to return. One bowl by the door where everything lands first. The pile still happens, but only in one place.

Your pile is not a character flaw. It is your memory, sitting outside your head. Dump the rest into my.adhd and get one thing back to clear.

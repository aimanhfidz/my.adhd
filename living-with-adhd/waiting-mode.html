Living with ADHD · Waiting mode

Why does one 4pm eat my whole day?

You are not wasting the day. You are guarding it.

The short version

01

You are on standby until 4

An appointment later today, and the hours before it go grey. You could start something. You do not. Anything you start might make you late.

02

Starting feels risky

If you get absorbed, you might lose track and miss it. So your brain plays safe and picks nothing. It looks like idleness. It is self-protection.

03

Waiting is still work

Checking the clock every ten minutes takes effort. By the time the appointment comes, you are tired from a day where nothing happened.

04

It comes from losing time before

After years of running late without meaning to, you stop trusting your sense of time. Waiting mode is your brain refusing to get caught out again.

05

Set two alarms, then let go

One for getting ready, one for leaving. Now watching the clock is the alarm's job, not yours, and the hours before are free again.

06

Pick a task with a clean stop

Laundry, a short email, a walk round the block. Things that end on their own are safer than a rabbit hole before 4pm.

07

Book things early when you can

A 9am appointment leaves the rest of the day open. Put errands on the same day, so the other days stay clear.

The waiting is real, and it is not laziness. Dump the day into my.adhd and get one thing back that fits before you go.

What my.adhd is

A tool for getting a cluttered head onto a list. You type what is in it, an AI sorts that into tasks with rough durations and a small first step, and the app shows you one thing at a time. That is the whole product.

The app is free to use and works without an account. Signing in is optional in the app, and required in one place only: the self-check, which keeps a result for you and therefore has to know whose it is.

We are not providing healthcare, the result is not a medical record, and the record we keep of it is not a clinical one — nobody reads your answers as a clinician, because nobody at MyADHD is acting as one. Take the score to a psychiatrist or a clinical psychologist, who are the only people who can actually diagnose ADHD.

Take the self-check

Sit with people who get it

A WhatsApp group for adults with ADHD, and for parents of kids who have it. Nobody here needs it explained.

Who it’s for

  • Adults with ADHD, diagnosed or not
  • Anyone still wondering, or waiting for an appointment
  • Parents raising a kid with ADHD

What it’s for

  • Asking “is it just me?” and hearing “no”
  • Swapping what actually works, small and specific
  • Company on a heavy day

Three things we ask

  • Be kind. Everyone here is carrying more than it looks.
  • What is shared in the group stays in the group.
  • No selling, and no medical advice. Share what worked for you, not what someone else should take.

Not a clinic

  • Nobody in the group can diagnose you or tell you what to take.
  • If today feels unsafe, Talian Kasih 15999 answers 24 hours, in Malay and English.

Come as you are. Reading quietly counts too.
